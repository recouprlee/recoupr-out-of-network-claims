# recoupr Out-of-Network Claims

A free skill for the AI assistant you already use. It reads your therapist's superbill and your plan documents, prepares your out-of-network claim or your appeal, and you submit it yourself. No account, no fee, no cut. Your health information never touches recoupr.

Skill folder and name field: `recoupr-out-of-network-claims`.


## What is included

Seven routines prepare a claim, check a superbill, read benefits, track a claim, prepare an appeal, make a client handout, and make a blank superbill template. You review and submit every item.
