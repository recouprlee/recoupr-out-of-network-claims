## Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Texas (HCSC), including ERS HealthSelect

Source: https://www.bcbstx.com/docs/forms/claim/tx/medical-claim-tx.pdf

Read date: 2026-10-09

### Research steps

1. Download the BCBSTX Claim Form and select `To pay Insured/Subscriber`. Complete every item and sign the member certification. https://www.bcbstx.com/docs/forms/claim/tx/medical-claim-tx.pdf
2. Attach the original provider bill. It must identify the provider or organization, patient, service date, service description, diagnosis code, and charge for each service. https://www.bcbstx.com/docs/forms/claim/tx/medical-claim-tx.pdf
3. Log in to Blue Access for Members and open the BAM Message Center. Create a new message. https://www.bcbstx.com/help-center/forms-and-documents/faqs
4. Attach the completed form and original provider bill. Select `Claims Submission Attachments` as the subject and click `Send`. https://www.bcbstx.com/help-center/forms-and-documents/faqs
5. HealthSelect uses this same route for medical and mental health out-of-network claims. https://healthselect.bcbstx.com/medical-benefits/claims
6. NOT PUBLISHED: accepted file types, per-file size, attachment count, one-PDF versus separate-file rule, and post-send reference. The HealthSelect guide says those values appear only in a signed-in pop-up. https://staging-healthselect.bcbstx.com/pdf/flier-oon-claims-guide.pdf
7. Check status under `Claims` in BAM or the BCBSTX Mobile App. BAM claim alerts are also available. https://www.bcbstx.com/help-center/claims-and-coverage/faqs

### Filing facts

- Portal upload: YES
- Portal: Blue Access for Members (BAM)
- Portal path: BAM Message Center > Create a new message > attach form and original provider bill > subject Claims Submission Attachments > Send
- Mail address: NOT PUBLISHED
- Fax: NOT PUBLISHED
- Attach: provider or organization name; patient name; date each service or supply was provided; description of each service or supply; diagnosis code; charge for each service or supply
- File types: NOT PUBLISHED; login pop-up is UNREADABLE
- File size limit: NOT PUBLISHED; login pop-up is UNREADABLE
- Maximum files: NOT PUBLISHED; login pop-up is UNREADABLE
- One file or separate files: NOT PUBLISHED
- Confirmation: NOT PUBLISHED
- Status check: BAM or BCBSTX Mobile App > Claims; BAM alerts are available; phone status is also published
- Processing time: NOT PUBLISHED
- Member deadline: Individual and family plans: end of the following calendar year; HealthSelect: 18 months from date of service; other group plans: NOT PUBLISHED
- Deadline basis: YES: individual and family, ERS HealthSelect, and employer or self-funded terms differ
- Mental health route: HealthSelect publishes the same BAM message route for medical and mental health; other employer carve-outs are NOT PUBLISHED

## TRICARE West (TriWest)

Source: https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2642.pdf

Read date: 2026-10-09

### Research steps

1. Portal upload is `NO` for a domestic Texas medical claim. TRICARE publishes online claim filing only for overseas claims. https://www.tricare.mil/PatientResources/Claims/MedicalClaims
2. Complete all 12 blocks of DD Form 2642, `Patient's Request for Medical Payment`, and sign it. https://www.tricare.mil/PatientResources/Claims/MedicalClaims
3. Attach a readable provider bill with the sponsor DoD ID or DBN, provider name and address, date and place of each service, description, charge for each service, and diagnosis. https://www.tricare.mil/PatientResources/Claims/MedicalClaims
4. Add the other insurer's EOB when other health insurance pays first. Add the injury form when the condition is accident or work related. https://www.tricare.mil/PatientResources/Claims/MedicalClaims
5. The member signature must be a physical wet signature or Common Access Card signature. A typed signature is not published as accepted. https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/DD/forms/dd/dd2642.pdf
6. No domestic upload step exists. The published domestic route is nonportal, so an upload-only recoupr package cannot be filed through the West portal. https://www.tricare.mil/PatientResources/Claims/MedicalClaims
7. Check claim status and EOBs in the TRICARE West Region Beneficiary Portal. https://www.tricare.mil/PatientResources/Claims/MedicalClaims

### Filing facts

- Portal upload: NO
- Portal: TRICARE West Region Beneficiary Portal, status only
- Portal path: Domestic West claims have no upload path; the official medical-claims page publishes nonportal submission and reserves online filing for overseas claims
- Mail address: NOT PUBLISHED
- Fax: NOT PUBLISHED
- Attach: sponsor DoD ID or DBN; provider name and address; date and place of each service; description of each service or supply; charge for each service; diagnosis; other-insurance EOB when applicable; proof of payment for overseas claims
- File types: NOT APPLICABLE
- File size limit: NOT APPLICABLE
- Maximum files: NOT APPLICABLE
- One file or separate files: NOT APPLICABLE
- Confirmation: NOT APPLICABLE
- Status check: TRICARE West Region Beneficiary Portal shows claim status and EOBs
- Processing time: NOT PUBLISHED
- Member deadline: United States and US territories: one year from service, or one year from inpatient discharge; overseas: three years from service; returned claims: original limit or 90 days from notice, whichever is later
- Deadline basis: YES: domestic, inpatient, overseas, and returned-claim rules differ
- Mental health route: Use DD 2642 and the domestic West claim route; no separate mental-health claim submission route is published
- Provider on file: YES: the provider must submit certification paperwork to become TRICARE-authorized; the claim is denied if the provider was not authorized on the service date
- TRICARE payment note: Member or beneficiary reimbursement after paying a nonparticipating provider; DD 2642 has no assignment-of-benefits field
- TRICARE provider recognition note: YES: the provider must submit certification paperwork to become TRICARE-authorized; the claim is denied if the provider was not authorized on the service date
