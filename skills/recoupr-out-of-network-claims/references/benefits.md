# Reading out-of-network benefits

Research read date: 2026-10-27

## 1. Summary of Benefits and Coverage

The Summary of Benefits and Coverage is the written summary required by 45 CFR
147.200 for the applicable benefit package. The exact regulation text below
controls the description.

| Topic | Exact regulation text | Plain words | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | "A group health plan (and its administrator as defined in section 3(16)(A) of ERISA)), and a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage, is required to provide a written summary of benefits and coverage (SBC) for each benefit package without charge to entities and individuals described in this paragraph (a)(1) in accordance with the rules of this section." | It is a no-charge written summary of a benefit package from the plan or issuer covered by this rule. | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/45/147.200 (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Group plan request | "A plan or issuer must provide the SBC to participants or beneficiaries upon request for an SBC or summary information about the health coverage, as soon as practicable, but in no event later than seven business days following receipt of the request." | A participant or beneficiary may request it. The outside limit is seven business days after receipt. | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/45/147.200 (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Individual coverage request | "A health insurance issuer offering individual health insurance coverage must provide an SBC to any individual or dependent upon request for an SBC or summary information about a health insurance product as soon as practicable, but in no event later than seven business days following receipt of the request." | An individual market issuer also has the seven business day outside limit after receipt. | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/45/147.200 (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Exceptions and limits | "The exceptions, reductions, and limitations of the coverage;" | The SBC must state coverage exceptions, reductions, and limitations. | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/45/147.200 (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Cost sharing | "The cost-sharing provisions of the coverage, including deductible, coinsurance, and copayment obligations;" | The SBC must state the deductible, coinsurance, and copayment obligations. | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/45/147.200 (Read date: 2026-10-27) |

The SBC is a summary. The person's plan document controls the complete terms.

## 3. Allowed amount

The therapist's charge and the plan's allowed amount are different numbers. The
plan calculates deductible and coinsurance against the covered allowed amount,
not automatically against the therapist's full charge. The plan document sets
the basis. When searching that document, look for `allowed amount`, `maximum
allowable`, `usual and customary`, `percent of Medicare`, and
`reference-based`.

| Point | Plain words | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Allowed amount | It is the maximum payment the plan will pay for a covered service. The plan may use another name for it. | https://www.cms.gov/files/document/uniform-glossary-english-060723.pdf (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Coinsurance base | Coinsurance is a percentage of the allowed amount. The person's share and the plan's share add to 100 percent after the deductible, subject to the plan terms. | eligibilityBenefitExplainer.ts; https://www.cms.gov/files/document/uniform-glossary-english-060723.pdf (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Charge above allowed amount | For out-of-network care, the person may owe the difference between the charge and allowed amount. The calculator keeps that difference in the person's expected cost. | https://www.cms.gov/files/document/uniform-glossary-english-060723.pdf (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Unknown basis | Estimate only when the person's document or member services answer states both the basis and the number. Otherwise the estimate is unknown. Ask question 5 below. | eligibilityBenefitExplainer.ts |

No percentage or dollar amount is treated as typical.

## 5. Estimate for one session

Supply every number. The coinsurance argument is the plan's share, not the
person's share. There are no defaults.

| Input | Meaning | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charge | What the therapist charged for the session | https://www.cms.gov/initiatives/your-patient-rights/medical-bill-rights/get-help/guides-resources/how-read-your-bill (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Allowed amount | The maximum covered amount stated by the plan for that service | https://www.cms.gov/files/document/uniform-glossary-english-060723.pdf (Read date: 2026-10-27) |
| Deductible remaining | How much of the applicable out-of-network deductible remains | eligibilityBenefitExplainer.ts |
| Coinsurance percent | The percentage of covered allowed cost the plan pays after deductible | eligibilityBenefitExplainer.ts |
| Out-of-pocket remaining | How much applicable out-of-network cost sharing remains before the stated limit | eligibilityBenefitExplainer.ts |

In words:

1. Use the lesser of the charge and allowed amount as the covered base.
2. Apply the lesser of that base, the deductible remaining, and the out-of-pocket remaining to the deductible.
3. Apply the plan share percentage to the covered amount left after the deductible.
4. If the person's allowed cost share would cross the supplied out-of-pocket remaining, cap that allowed cost share there and increase the plan payment for the rest of the covered base.
5. The person's expected cost is the charge minus the expected plan payment. The amount above the allowed amount remains in that cost but does not reduce the supplied out-of-pocket remaining in this arithmetic.

Worked arithmetic with labeled sentinel numbers:

```text
SENTINEL_CHARGE_1: 200.00
SENTINEL_ALLOWED_AMOUNT_1: 120.00
SENTINEL_DEDUCTIBLE_REMAINING_1: 50.00
SENTINEL_PLAN_SHARE_PERCENT_1: 70
SENTINEL_OUT_OF_POCKET_REMAINING_1: 80.00

covered base: 120.00
amount applied to deductible: 50.00
covered amount after deductible: 70.00
plan expected payment: 49.00
person expected cost: 151.00
remaining deductible: 0.00
remaining out-of-pocket: 9.00
```

Run the same arithmetic with:

```text
python3 scripts/estimate_benefit.py CHARGE ALLOWED_AMOUNT DEDUCTIBLE_REMAINING PLAN_SHARE_PERCENT OUT_OF_POCKET_REMAINING
```

Honest limits:

- This arithmetic does not decide whether the service is covered.
- It does not guess an allowed amount, deductible, coinsurance, or out-of-pocket number.
- It cannot include a copay, visit limit, authorization rule, excluded service, or another plan rule not represented by the five inputs.
- The plan document must say which costs count toward its out-of-network limit. If that is unclear, the estimate is unknown.
- The result is an estimate, not a promise of payment.
